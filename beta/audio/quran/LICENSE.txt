Maher al-Muaiqly — King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex recording

Human recitation. The original MP3 excerpts remain 96 kbps / 44.1 kHz / stereo, without transcoding.
Faisal accepted the twelve verse excerpts on 11 October 2026 and directly requested
the two joined pairs. He separately listened to and approved the Basmala twice.
The joined pairs retain all original frames and pauses between the accepted excerpts.

On 13 October Faisal requested that the Basmala and verses play as one connected file.
The ten playback files in joined/ use FLAC, preserving the concatenated independently
decoded 16-bit / 44.1 kHz / stereo PCM without added or removed samples, fades or resampling.
This file conversion adds no generated speech and does not improve the original MP3 quality.
The approved MP3 files and their original provenance remain unchanged. Exact connected-file
hashes and decoded-sample verification are recorded separately in joined-provenance.json.
