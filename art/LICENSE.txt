Constellation drawings in this folder are modified versions of the illustrations of Stellarium's
"modern" sky culture (artwork originally by Johan Meuris, Stellarium team):
https://github.com/stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/modern

Modification: redrawn as light line art (outline and inner strokes) and converted to WebP by Najm.
