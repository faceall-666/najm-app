Constellation figures in this folder are modified versions of the illustrations of Stellarium's
"modern" sky culture (artwork originally by Johan Meuris, Stellarium team):
https://github.com/stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/modern

Modification: each figure was redrawn as an antique star-atlas engraving with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5)
using the original as reference, aligned back onto the original silhouette, and converted by Najm
(scripts/build_art.py) into light line art:
  sheets/<name>.webp  the sky's figure: contour + soft fill, inner lines, hatching, each with its mip levels
  atlas.webp          every figure's outline picture ("All figures")
  <name>.webp         the constellation page's picture (contour, hatching and fill)

15 October 2026: shaded/<name>.png holds byte-identical original Johan Meuris figures from
data-src/stellarium-modern/illustrations (83 files, original128/256/512 pixel frames).
The selected sky figure, detail picture and list image use this smooth shaded artwork rather than dense hatching.
They remain under Free Art License1.3. No anchor, crop or silhouette was moved; rendering tint/intensity is applied
at runtime. Andromeda and Indus retain the existing fully clothed derived outlines. The All atlas stays outline art.
Reproduce these copies with scripts/build_art.py shaded. The existing engraved sheets remain for legacy/reference
and outline support; regenerating those does not overwrite shaded/.
