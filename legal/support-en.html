Support

How can we help?

Publisher: Luna Sky LLC. Contact the Najm Sky team at contact@12lunasky.com for help, problem reports or privacy requests. This page has no automatic submission form. Last updated: 23 October 2026.

Get started

Tap Start exploring and choose your city, or allow location when needed. Drag the sky to browse manually; switch Camera on for the live view if you want it. Check the place/time being used, particularly after changing dates or when marked approximate.

Heading, camera and permissions

Device settings control camera, location, motion and notifications. Declining camera/location does not prevent manual browsing. If you chose manual mode, use Return to tracking to resume sensors. Move away from metal or magnetic objects when calibrating heading and check city/time before reporting an object-position difference.

Audio and journeys

Start narration using the journey’s playback control. Opening Read pauses narration; exiting stops playback. Returning does not restart it automatically. Check media volume and speaker/Bluetooth routing. Narration ends at its authored stop: complete the required interaction, then select Continue. The chapter menu lets you jump or reopen the tool guide where available.

The native bundle contains core sky data and recordings, but some media and current orbit updates need internet access. If a file fails, check the connection and open it again. The app generates no new audio during use.

Plans, reminders and journal

Plans, places, favourites and journal records stay locally, with no Najm-owned account or sync. Deleting a plan/site does not delete its historical journal copy. Cancel unwanted reminders using reminder controls. Presentation also depends on system permission, Focus, power management and notification settings. Do not rely on reminders for emergency or second-precise alerts.

Images and sharing

Review the preview and its place/time footer before saving or sending. On iOS, saving adds the image without reading your library. If completion is unknown, check Photos or the chosen destination before retrying: an operation already handed to the system may finish after the preview closes. Browser copies use the browser’s supported share/download features.

Free v1 launch

The v1 launch and all features included in it are free. No purchase, subscription or Najm account is needed. There is no purchase or subscription to restore in this launch version. Never send support a password or payment credentials.

Report a problem

Include the build ID from app information, device model, OS version, language and brief steps to reproduce it. Say whether Camera/manual mode was used and whether you were online. Add a screenshot only if useful, after removing information you do not want to share.

Diagnostics may include location, sensor readings or alignment images. Do not send a full report or precise coordinates unless needed and deliberately chosen. Support cannot remotely read or erase your device-local records. See Privacy for deletion choices and data requests.

Observe safely

Najm is for exploration and education, not weather, navigation or emergencies. Never view the Sun directly or through optics without suitable solar protection. See the Terms of Use for service scope and content rights.
