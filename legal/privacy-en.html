Privacy Policy

About this policy

Publisher: Luna Sky LLC. This policy explains how Najm Sky handles your data on iOS and Android, including optional network services and contact with support. Last updated: 23 October 2026.

Location, camera and sensors

Najm calculates the sky for the place and time you choose. You can select a city manually and drag the sky without granting location or camera access. If you choose device location, the app uses coordinates, accuracy information and the time zone for horizon and observing-time calculations, and saves the selected place locally. Time-zone lookup uses bundled data rather than sending coordinates to a time-zone service.

Rear-camera frames and motion/heading readings align the sky on your device. Najm does not automatically upload these frames, readings or observing notes to a Najm server, record microphone audio or use background location.

What stays on your device

Local records include display/language/place preferences, saved sites, favourites, plans, observing journal entries, journey progress, content choices and guidance preferences. A journal entry may contain targets, dates, places, outcomes and notes you choose to save. Entries keep independent copies of plan/place information: deleting the original does not remove historical entries.

The covered version requires no Najm account and provides no Najm-owned cloud sync for these records. Questions and answers in Najma stay in the local guide session; they are not sent to a conversational model or voice-generation service. This version contains no advertising, advertising tracking or behavioural analytics transmission.

Reminders, images and exports

On supported devices and builds, you may allow notifications and confirm an observing reminder. Its plan title and time stay on the device; the title may appear on the lock screen. These are local reminders, not cloud push. You can cancel them using reminder controls and change permission in device settings.

A screenshot, report or calendar file is shared only when you choose to export or share it. A screenshot may contain camera imagery, an object name, place and time. Diagnostics may contain coordinates, device/display details, heading readings and alignment snapshots. Review the file first. The destination you select receives it under its own controls. Temporary export files may remain until app or system cleanup; no fixed deletion deadline is promised.

On iOS, Save to Photos requests add-only access rather than permission to read your library. Saved and shared copies follow the destination and your chosen backup/sync settings.

Connections and external services

Current satellite orbit updates use CelesTrak. Requests contain fixed group/catalogue identifiers, not your selected place or notes. The native bundle includes core sky data and recordings, but some media is fetched on demand from Najm’s media host. The website and hosted media use najem.space0cast.com, served from GitHub Pages through a proxied Cloudflare security gateway. Requests to that domain pass through this gateway, including any resources requested there by the native app.

Network services receive connection data needed to deliver files, such as IP address, User-Agent, requested resource and request information. The security gateway uses request metadata for access protection and may retain a bounded security record after a direct security-trap request or repeated failed owner authentication. That record can include IP address, User-Agent, path without the query, and available country, network organization and TLS information. This does not add a cookie banner, advertising tracker or device fingerprint to the native app; its resource requests do not attach camera frames, your selected coordinates or observing notes. Hosting providers manage their own logs under their practices; this page promises no common retention period for every provider. “Local data” does not mean the network or operating system records nothing. See CelesTrak, Cloudflare privacy and GitHub privacy.

Opening an external source takes you to a service with its own privacy policy. External pages do not receive the app’s native permissions. We do not use observing records for advertising or sell them.

Free v1 launch

The v1 launch is free, with all features included in that version available without a purchase, subscription or Najm account. This launch version offers no production purchase service and sends no purchase receipts or account keys to a Najm billing server. We will update this policy before introducing a purchase service, account or cloud sync in a later version.

Support correspondence

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the message/attachments you choose to send through the email service. We use them to answer and follow up on your request, not for advertising. Do not send passwords, payment details, private images or precise location unless you deliberately need to share them. Avoid unnecessary children’s personal data when contacting us on a child’s behalf.

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle and follow up on the request and meet applicable legal or security obligations. You can ask support to delete or correct it; we will explain any information that must be retained and why. Support cannot remotely access or erase your device-local records.

Choices, retention and deletion

You can revoke permissions in device settings, change city and delete favourites, plans, sites and journal entries using their in-app controls. Local data remains until you delete it or remove app data under system controls. In a browser, removing site data may erase cached files and preferences. Keep information you need first.

Exported copies and backups managed by your operating system or chosen services may remain; remove them through that destination’s controls. This version has no single erase-everything button or cloud account requiring closure. You may contact us about data we control to request information, correction or deletion and exercise rights available under applicable law.

Security, contact and updates

We use system permissions, app storage and HTTPS connections to reduce unintended access. No method guarantees absolute security, so review exports before sharing. When data use changes, we update this page/date and provide any required additional disclosure or consent before the new use.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Najm Sky team at contact@12lunasky.com, or see Support.
