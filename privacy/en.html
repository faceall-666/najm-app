Privacy and cookies — Najm Sky

Website edition · Updated18October2026

Use Najm without an account

The website currently requires no Najm account or sign-in and has no cart or production checkout. Owner security administration is separate; visitors are not asked for Google Authenticator.

Sky data on your device

Local records include language, city, display settings, favourites, observing sites, plans, journal entries, journey progress, content and guidance choices. A service worker caches files for offline use. The new analytics module does not receive camera imagery, coordinates or observing notes. You can decline camera/location and choose a city and browse manually.

Cookie choices

Necessary storage runs the site and remembers the preferences and files you requested. Analytics/performance and marketing/social platforms are separate optional categories, off by default. Accept optional, choose necessary only, or customize either category without losing access to the sky or journeys.

Retention and withdrawal

The first-party najm-consent-v1 cookie stores your choice for up to365days without a visitor identifier. Beta has a separate name and scope. Browsers may shorten retention or remove cookies earlier. Use Privacy and cookies to change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal stops event dispatch, removes accessible first-party cookies and reloads when a tool was loaded; it cannot recall previously transmitted data. Manage third-party cookies through browser settings as well.

Optional analytics and platforms

Supported tools are Google Analytics4, Meta for Instagram/Facebook, TikTok, Snap and X. A tool loads only when its owner-configured identifier is enabled and you consent to its category. All identifiers and optional transmission are disabled in the current default configuration. If activated with consent, the provider can receive connection/browser details, measurement cookies and events under its policy, potentially outside your country. This module passes no email, telephone, journal, search phrase or coordinates and does not automatically enable advanced matching. No fictional cart or purchase events are sent; those functions do not exist here.

Visit source

Only after marketing consent, najm-source-v1 may remember a coarse source such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Telegram for30days. It contains a source name, medium category and capture time, never a referrer URL, username, free-form campaign text or click identifier. This is first-touch attribution; opening the site does not extend its window. It is not currently linked to purchases/accounts because neither exists.

First-party statistics

When the Najm server is activated and analytics consent is granted, fixed event names can be accumulated into daily counters without a user/device identifier or IP in the statistics table. Declining consent and supported browser privacy signals stop transmission. Hosting/network systems still receive connection data needed to deliver requests. Measurement cannot cover every visit or bypass browser blocking.

Hosting and security

The current website uses GitHub Pages and CloudflareDNS. Hosting and external content services such as CelesTrak receive connection data necessary for delivery. The new administrative gateway is prepared locally; its files do not establish a live edge ban. When deployed, bounded security request data is isolated from visitor analytics to prevent abuse. No device fingerprint is collected inside the mobile app.

Sharing and control

Notes/plans stay local until you choose to export or share. Clearing site data may remove settings and records; retain what you need first. Observing notes are not sold or passed to advertising pixels. Shared copies follow the selected destination and your choices.

Mobile apps

This page covers the website. iOS and Android artifacts exclude the cookie banner, pixels, administration and honeypots. They currently request no IDFA and perform no advertising tracking. Future activation requires official tools, disclosures and applicable permission; web consent never grants mobileATT permission.

Contact and final review

For privacy questions/requests: contact@najem.com, as listed in the approved policy packet. Operational verification of this mailbox, publisher details and final disclosures remain necessary before tracking activation or Store submission. This page does not promise absolute compliance in every jurisdiction or guaranteed Store acceptance.
